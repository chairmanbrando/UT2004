# CoD Mode

**For when you simply don't feel like managing your health pool.™**

How it works is simple enough:

- Go 4 seconds without taking damage and your health starts regenerating back to 100.
- The default regeneration rate is 25/second. In the worst case you're out of the action for 8 seconds.
- Health chargers, no longer being particularly useful, now hold a less-mini health vial worth +15.

The no-damage-taken wait time and regeneration rate are configurable, though, so you can recreate your favorite of the 47 CoD games.

## But wait, there's more!

#### Low-brain mode!

A configuration option called _Loadout Provided_ will grant everyone a Rocket Launcher and Lightning Gun on spawn, each with full ammo. Your Assault Rifle is still there but only has two grenades. The idea is to mimic a CoD loadout, of course.

All weapon bases (except super weapons) and all ammo pickups are gone from the map. Your only source of additional ammo is from dropped weapons, each of which gives you the ammo a pickup would have granted -- e.g. 9 for the Rocket Launcher.

## Future possibilities...

#### No-brain mode!

For when you really don't want to bother playing the game, all health and armor pickups are also removed from the map. You spawn with and can regenerate to 150 health to compensate. Just like in CoD, a headshot on a fresh spawn is only sometimes a kill. 🙃

Additionally removed from the map is, well, every other thing you could pick up and could make particular areas of the map more important than others. After all, the core of CoD gameplay insists that no one should have to make decisions or worry about map positioning. It's "not fair" when someone uses anything other than their aim to kill you.

You shoot what's right in front of you, die, respawn, and repeat. Welcome to CoD, baby.
