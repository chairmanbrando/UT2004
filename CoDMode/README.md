# CoD Mode

**For when you simply don't feel like managing your health pool.™**

How it works is simple enough:

- Go 4 seconds without taking damage and your health starts regenerating back to 100.
- The default regeneration rate is 25/second. So, in the worst case you're out of the action for 8 seconds.
- Health chargers, no longer being particularly useful, now hold a less-mini health vial worth +15.

The no-damage wait time, regeneration rate, and stopping point for the regeneration are all configurable.

I *might* try to add one of those red overlay rings around the screen edges that grows as you're damaged to really drive home the CoDness. Probably not, though.
